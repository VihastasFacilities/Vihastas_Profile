Who We Are

About Vihastas

Vihastas is a workspace solutions company dedicated to transforming raw spaces into fully functional, aesthetically inspiring, and technologically integrated work environments. We handle interiors, turnkey fitouts, and critical infrastructure so our clients get a single, accountable partner from concept to completion.

Our focus is on designing and delivering offices that enhance productivity, reflect brand personality, and meet the operational realities of growing businesses.

What We Do

From design development and space planning to site execution and handover, Vihastas manages every stage of workspace creation. We coordinate civil, MEP, and IT requirements to ensure that aesthetics and functionality work together seamlessly.
