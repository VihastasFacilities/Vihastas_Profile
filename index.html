About Vihastas

Vihastas is a workspace solutions company dedicated to transforming raw spaces into fully functional, aesthetically inspiring, and technologically integrated work environments. We handle interiors, turnkey fitouts, and critical infrastructure so our clients get a single, accountable partner from concept to completion.

Our focus is on designing and delivering offices that enhance productivity, reflect brand personality, and meet the operational realities of growing businesses.

What We Do

From design development and space planning to site execution and handover, Vihastas manages every stage of workspace creation. We coordinate civil, MEP, and IT requirements to ensure that aesthetics and functionality work together seamlessly.

Our Services

Vihastas offers integrated services that cover every layer of a modern workspace.

Interior Design & Planning Professional interior design and layout planning tailored to workspace requirements, user comfort, and brand identity.
Turnkey Fitouts Complete fitout solutions including partitions, ceilings, flooring, joinery, and architectural modifications.
Server Rooms & Data Areas Specialized design and setup for server rooms with proper cooling, access, and security infrastructure.
Centralised AC Systems Design, installation, and optimisation of centralized air‑conditioning and HVAC for efficient climate control.
Electrical & Lighting Complete electrical planning, distribution, lighting, and power management meeting safety and compliance standards.
Sensors & Automation Integration of sensors and smart systems for monitoring, security, energy efficiency, and automated workspace management.
Furniture Solutions Curated furniture and custom installations that combine ergonomics, functionality, and modern aesthetics.
Graphics & Branding Environmental graphics, signage, and branding elements that reinforce company identity throughout the workspace.
